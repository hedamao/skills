# Design System

## Overview

Ping An e生保 is presented as a mobile-first health insurance purchase flow. The page uses a warm orange hero banner, a family photograph, white and pale-gray information cards, and a persistent orange conversion button. The visual tone is direct, trustworthy, and promotional, with high-contrast benefit numbers and compact Chinese mobile UI patterns.

## Colors

- **Primary Text**: `#15161A` — near-black for core product information.
- **Primary Surface**: `#FFFFFF` — white card and modal surfaces.
- **CTA Orange**: `#FF7C0A` — main action, selected states, emphasis.
- **Deep Orange**: `#FF6600` — secondary CTA emphasis and glow.
- **Trust Blue**: `#5586FF` — insurance/service accent.
- **Warm Cream**: `#FFF7F2` — soft disclaimer and promo surfaces.
- **Muted Gray**: `#969799` — supporting metadata.

## Typography

- **Primary Sans**: `-apple-system`, `system-ui`, `Helvetica Neue`, `PingFang SC`, `Microsoft Yahei`, sans-serif. Used for all UI labels, body copy, and CTA text.
- **Display Weight**: 700 for major benefit numbers and title cards.
- **Body Weight**: 400 to 500 for detail text and legal/supporting copy.

## Elevation

The page relies on mobile card layering rather than heavy shadows. Rounded white cards sit over warm orange imagery, with subtle gray borders and selected orange outlines. Depth in the promo should come from the phone mockup, soft orange glows, and card parallax.

## Components

- **Hero Insurance Banner**: Family photo with orange promotional background and benefit badge overlays.
- **Plan Selector**: Two-column mobile segmented control with orange selected border.
- **Benefit Grid**: Compact cards showing 400万, 200万, and disease/drug protection lines.
- **Disclosure Modal**: White legal confirmation sheet with orange pill CTA.
- **Sticky CTA Bar**: Bottom purchase action with `立即投保`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use the captured mobile screenshot as the recognizable product surface.
- Keep CTA orange visible in every beat.
- Use large Chinese benefit figures: `400万`, `200万`, `15%`.
- Preserve a clean insurance-product feel with white cards and restrained shadows.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the brand into a dark-tech visual system.
- Do not replace the product UI with generic medical illustrations.
- Do not bury compliance/disclosure language; show it as part of the trusted flow.
- Do not use unrelated colors outside the orange, white, gray, and blue palette.
